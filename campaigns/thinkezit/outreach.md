## Operating rules
- One verified personalization fact minimum per message.
- Do not claim or infer a vulnerability from public characteristics.
- Do not repeat the expired October 3, 2025 promotional deadline/scarcity language on ThinkEzIT's legacy trial page.
- Human review before pricing, contractual terms, compliance claims or bespoke security assertions.
- Suppress opt-outs, bounces and duplicates across sequences.
- Record prospect, score, vertical, owner, Jab 1 date, Jab 2 date, Hook date, reply, meeting, disposition and suppression state.
- Every metric must route to an action; do not create a report with no consumer.
