## Objective
Turn one ThinkEzIT capability set into two materially different buyer journeys—legal and healthcare—using Jab-Jab-Hook. Every artifact must be useful on its own and have a downstream consumer/action.

## Funnel
1. Outreach or QR enters the vertical page with campaign/source UTM attribution.
2. Prospect receives immediate value through the vertical readiness check; no form gate.
3. Personalized Dr. Max Justice video explains why the verticalized conversation matters to Michael and Barry/ThinkEzIT.
4. Jab #2 gives a materially different operational worksheet.
5. Hook offers ThinkEzIT's current EZ 30-Day Free Trial for one appropriate priority desktop/laptop, or contact for a scoped dependency review.
6. ThinkEzIT's own form/phone is the conversion endpoint; this campaign does not create a second CRM/form authority plane.

## Measurement
Track page entry/source, readiness-check interaction, Jab #1 open, Jab #2 open, video start and quartiles where supported, trial click, contact click, replies, meetings, disposition and suppression. Metrics are useful only when they route to a campaign owner and next action.

## Video requirement

## Definition of done
A reviewer can enter from a tracked legal or healthcare URL, use both jabs, watch the real personalized video, reach a working ThinkEzIT conversion endpoint, and verify the journey on mobile and desktop without assistance. No 404s, placeholder URLs, hidden implementation notes, fake scarcity, unverified security claims or orphan metrics.
