Healthcare Cyber Resilience Decision Exercise

Could leadership make the right decisions while critical clinical systems are unavailable?

Use this executive exercise to test decision ownership, continuity, recovery evidence, third-party dependency, patient-safety escalation, and AI/data boundaries without submitting PHI or sensitive system information.

Scenario: a material EHR or clinical-system outage occurs while a critical third-party service is also degraded and patient care must continue. The point is not to predict the incident. The point is to test whether authority and evidence are clear before it happens.
No PHI or sensitive system data. Do not enter patient identifiers, medical records, credentials, IP addresses, remote-access endpoints, vulnerability output, EHR configuration, network diagrams, vendor secrets, or active incident evidence.

Use these decisions in your next leadership discussion.

  • Who can invoke downtime procedures and when?
  • How long can safe manual operations continue?
  • Who decides when cyber isolation outweighs operational disruption?
  • What evidence is required before systems reconnect?
  • When does a cyber event become a patient-safety, executive, legal, or privacy escalation?
  • Who owns failure of a critical third-party dependency?
