Seven questions that change the client AI conversation

Use this map before discussing models, licenses or features. Identify the decision, consequence and evidence requirement first.

Question 1

Which business decision will this AI influence?

If the answer is only “productivity,” keep drilling until the actual decision or action is explicit.

Question 2

What is the consequence if the recommendation is wrong?

Separate reversible inconvenience from financial, security, legal, safety, customer or reputational harm.

Question 3

What evidence must exist before we trust the recommendation?

Define acceptable sources, freshness, corroboration and confidence before the output arrives.

Question 4

What may the AI recommend, approve or execute?

Capability and authority are different. Make the authority boundary explicit.

Question 5

Where must a human intervene—and can that person meaningfully stop the action?

A ceremonial approval after the decision is effectively irreversible is not meaningful oversight.

Question 6

What changes would invalidate our previous trust decision?

Model, vendor, data, workflow, policy and authority changes can make yesterday's assurance stale.

Question 7

What record will prove why this decision was allowed?

The final record should connect claims, evidence, consequence, authority, review and disposition.

Conversation pivot: “Before we decide which AI to deploy, let’s decide which decisions we’re willing to trust it with—and what evidence we’ll require before we do.”
