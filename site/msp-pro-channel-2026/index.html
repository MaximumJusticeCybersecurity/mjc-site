Three questions MSPs need answered before an AI recommendation becomes consequential.

01

What evidence supported it?

Can you reconstruct the claims, sources, confidence and assumptions behind the recommendation?

02

Who had authority?

Was the AI permitted to recommend, approve or execute—and was human review meaningful before consequence?

03

What happens if it is wrong?

Do controls change as Risk If Wrong rises, or does every AI output receive essentially the same trust?

The technology is not the decision.
