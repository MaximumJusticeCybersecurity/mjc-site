AI Decision Assurance Readiness Checklist

For each item you cannot confidently answer “yes,” count one gap. The objective is not to prove AI is safe. It is to determine whether your organization can defend the decisions it allows AI to influence.

We maintain an inventory of consequential decisions materially influenced by AI.
We can reconstruct the evidence, claims, assumptions and confidence supporting a material AI recommendation.
AI authority is explicitly bounded; technical capability is never treated as permission.
Risk If Wrong determines when meaningful human review is mandatory.
Unsupported claims and uncertainty are distinguishable before an AI recommendation becomes action.
Material model, vendor, data-source or workflow changes trigger reassessment.
Material AI-supported actions produce a defensible decision record.
There is an accountable human owner for the consequence if the decision fails.
Interpretation
0–2 gaps: validate control effectiveness and evidence quality.
3–4 gaps: conduct a targeted Decision Assurance review.
5+ gaps: treat the issue as executive governance risk before expanding AI authority.

Take this question back to the team: If this AI-supported decision caused material harm tomorrow, what evidence would we show an executive, customer, regulator, insurer or attorney to explain why we trusted it?
