Before you score anything, challenge the evidence.

A Yes should mean more than “we believe this is handled.” Ask what evidence an auditor, regulator, insurer, governing board, or executive could see today that proves the answer is actually Yes. If the evidence cannot be produced, treat the answer as Partial and recalculate.

One NO can matter more than nine YES answers. A single uncontrolled remote pathway or inability to sustain essential operations can materially change the decision even when the total score looks low.
