Jab #2 · Provider Validation / Executive Evidence Worksheet

Seven questions your utility should be able to answer — with evidence.

  1. Can we produce a current inventory of every remote-access and internet-facing pathway into IT and OT?
  2. Can we show evidence that privileged and remote access is MFA-protected, attributable, reviewed, and revoked when no longer required?
  3. Can we restore PLC, HMI, and network configurations from tested known-good copies?
  4. Can essential water or wastewater operations continue safely when critical digital systems are unavailable?
  5. Do we know what data our vendors, managed-service providers, cloud tools, and AI services can receive from us?
  6. Can leadership identify who has authority to isolate systems, invoke manual operations, notify regulators or law enforcement, and authorize restoration?
  7. What independent evidence proves the controls we believe are working actually work?

For every question, capture: Owner | Evidence | Last Tested | Gap | Next Action. A Yes should mean evidence can be produced, not merely that everyone believes the control is handled.

The objective is to make assurance visible and decision-useful while strengthening the conversation with your incumbent provider, not replacing that provider.
