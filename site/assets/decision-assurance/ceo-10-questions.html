The CEO's 10 Questions Before Acting on an AI Recommendation

Use these questions before an AI-generated or AI-influenced recommendation can create material financial, operational, security, safety, compliance, access, or reputational consequences.

1. Which AI-generated recommendations can move money, change access, affect safety, create regulatory exposure, or alter mission outcomes?
2. Which AI outputs can become actions without meaningful human review?
3. What evidence must be present before a high-consequence recommendation is admissible?
4. What stops or escalates a decision when evidence is missing, stale, contradictory, or unverifiable?
5. Can we distinguish model confidence from confidence in the business decision?
6. How does Risk If Wrong change our evidence threshold and review requirement?
7. Who has accountable authority to approve, reject, condition, or override the recommendation?
8. Can an AI agent exceed the authority intended by policy, credentials, tools, or workflow composition?
9. Could we reconstruct why a consequential AI decision was made six months later?
10. If a regulator, customer, board member, auditor, or plaintiff challenged the decision tomorrow, what exact record would we produce?
The MJC Decision Assurance loop: Claims → Evidence → Consequences / Risk If Wrong → Decision → AI Trust Decision Record.
