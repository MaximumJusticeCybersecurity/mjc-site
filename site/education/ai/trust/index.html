Education AI Trust Toolkit

How AI-ready is your school when the answer sounds confident but the evidence is weak?

Use the Five-Question AI Trust Test first, then complete a short readiness diagnostic across governance, privacy, cybersecurity, academic integrity, evidence validation, human oversight, and leadership accountability.

The Five-Question AI Trust Test: 1) What material claim is the AI making? 2) What evidence supports it? 3) What evidence or context is missing? 4) What happens if it is wrong? 5) Which accountable human has authority to decide?
Protect student and security information. Do not enter student or education records, medical or disability information, disciplinary records, credentials, protected identifiers, network weaknesses, vulnerability output, or other sensitive school-security information.

Try the Trust Test before the diagnostic.

Take one AI-generated recommendation your school has seen, even a harmless example. Separate the claim from the evidence. Identify what is missing. Name the consequence if wrong. Then identify who actually owns the decision. This is the habit the diagnostic is designed to operationalize.

AI fluency is not evidence. A polished answer can still be incomplete, stale, unsupported, or outside the authority of the person receiving it.
