Six questions worth answering before the next outage

Count one gap for each statement you cannot confidently answer “yes” to today.

We know the expected recovery time for the systems attorneys depend on most.
Email, remote access and privileged systems use modern identity controls beyond passwords alone.
We can explain where sensitive client information resides and who can access it.
Operating systems and common applications are patched proactively.
Someone will know quickly if a backup, endpoint control, firewall or priority workstation stops working.
The firm knows how client work continues if internet, phones, hardware or a primary system becomes unavailable.
Interpretation
0–1 gaps: validate the controls and keep testing.
2–3 gaps: prioritize the dependencies that consume the most attorney time or create the greatest client consequence.
4+ gaps: build a short continuity plan before waiting for an outage to reveal the priorities.

Take this back to the partners: If our most important system failed at 10:00 tomorrow morning, what would attorneys do at 10:05—and who owns recovery?
