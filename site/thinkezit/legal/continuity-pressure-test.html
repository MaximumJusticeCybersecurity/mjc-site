One-System Continuity Pressure Test

Pick the one technology dependency that would consume the most attorney/staff time if it became unavailable tomorrow. Complete this before discussing products.

1. System/dependency:
2. What client work stops if it fails?
3. Maximum tolerable downtime:
4. Who owns recovery?
5. What is the manual workaround?
6. What evidence proves recovery will work?
7. When was that recovery path last tested?

Decision rule: if the business consequence is high and the recovery path is assumed rather than tested, that dependency deserves validation before the next incident.
