Downtime consumes billable time. Email compromise can become client and financial risk. Lost access to case information can collide with deadlines. The right technology conversation starts with those consequences rather than a catalog of products.

2-minute law-firm technology check

How many can you answer “yes” to?

Recovery

Do you know how quickly attorneys regain access if a critical workstation, server, or cloud account fails?

Email & identity

Are email, remote access, and privileged systems protected by modern identity controls rather than passwords alone?

Confidentiality

Can the firm explain who can access sensitive client information, where it resides, and how access is controlled?

Updates

Are operating systems and applications patched proactively across the firm?

Visibility

Would someone know quickly if a firewall, backup, endpoint control, or critical workstation stopped working?

Continuity

Can the firm continue serving clients when internet, phones, hardware, or a primary system becomes unavailable?

5–6 yes: validate and maintain.   3–4: prioritize the gaps.   0–2: avoidable operational exposure deserves attention.
