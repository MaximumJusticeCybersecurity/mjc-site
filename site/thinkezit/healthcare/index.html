Technology readiness for healthcare practices

When care depends on technology, “the system is down” becomes an operational problem immediately.

Healthcare technology has to support availability, privacy, recovery, secure access, communications, and the daily clinical workflow. The question is not how much technology you own. It is whether it works when patients and staff need it.

Take the 2-minute readiness check
2-minute healthcare technology check

Pressure-test the systems behind the practice.

Clinical availability

Can staff continue essential operations when a workstation, network component, internet connection, or cloud service fails?

Identity & access

Are accounts protected appropriately, access removed promptly when roles change, and privileged access limited?

Recovery

Are backups monitored and recovery expectations understood before an outage or ransomware event forces the test?

Endpoints

Are workstations and applications inventoried, patched, monitored, and protected consistently?

Communications

Are phones, connectivity, email, and other essential communications designed with continuity in mind?

Third parties

Does the practice understand which technology vendors touch sensitive information or critical workflows and how those dependencies are managed?
