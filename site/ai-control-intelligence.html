The problem

Static governance is not enough when AI can affect consequential decisions.

Policies and control catalogs matter, but a consequential AI decision also depends on what the system is allowed to do, what evidence supports the action now, what happens if it is wrong, and whether the execution conditions still hold at the moment of effect.

Authority

Technical capability does not equal permission. AI authority needs explicit scope, limits, revocation, and accountable ownership.

Evidence

Confidence is not evidence. Material decisions need current, relevant, attributable support that matches the consequence.

Consequence

Controls should become stronger as Risk If Wrong rises, especially for financial, security, regulatory, operational, safety, or reputational effects.
